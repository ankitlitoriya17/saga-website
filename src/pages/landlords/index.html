03 / FOLLOW THE RECORD, MONTH BY MONTH

Rent tracking.
With review built in.

See tenant-wise rent history and outstanding dues. Keep payment submissions distinct from landlord-approved records.

  1. Record

    Record rent payments for a month, or review a tenant’s payment submission.

  2. Review

    Approve payment records after checking them. A pending submission is not an approved payment.

  3. Track

    Review rent, advances and electricity charges. Generate and share a rent receipt when the payment record is eligible.

SPoT tracks payment records; it is not an online rent payment gateway. Arrange and confirm the actual payment directly with your tenant.

Landlord questions.

What can landlords do with SPoT?+

Use the Android app to create rental property listings, manage units and availability, review applications, organise tenant records and track rent payment records.

Can I list a flat, room, PG or shop for rent?+

Yes. SPoT supports these property types. Add the relevant property and unit details, photos and amenities, then review before publishing. Listing a property does not guarantee enquiries or a tenant.

Can I add tenants who did not apply through SPoT?+

Yes. The owner’s Add Tenant flow lets you enter tenant details and assign an available unit. Accepting a rental application alone does not start a tenancy.

Does SPoT collect rent or transfer money?+

No. Recording or reporting rent creates a payment record for tracking and review. SPoT is not an online rent payment gateway. Confirm the actual payment separately; a tenant submission remains pending until landlord approval.

Can I track advances, electricity and rent receipts?+

The app includes advance payment records, electricity readings and charges, and rent payment history. Rent receipts are available for eligible landlord-approved payment records.

Kya main kirayedar aur kiraye ka hisaab rakh sakta hoon?+

Haan. Property mein tenant add karke monthly rent, payment records, advance aur electricity ki details manage karein. Payment report ko verify karke approve karein; record banana paise transfer karna nahi hai.

Do I need to submit my details on this website?+

No. This is a static introduction to the Android app, with illustrative screenshots. This page has no forms and uses no cookies, analytics or tracking. Read the app privacy policy before signing in to SPoT. For account support, see Delete my account or contact support.
