Talk to the team {{icon:arrow}}
What can I find on SPoT?+

SPoT supports homes, rooms, PGs and shops. Listings and availability depend on what owners publish in your selected city. Explore current options in the Android app.

Can I manage my property as an owner?+

Yes. The Android app includes property and unit management, applications, tenant records, rent and electricity tracking, and rent receipt sharing.

Does reporting rent transfer money?+

Reporting or recording rent creates a payment record for review and tracking. It does not itself transfer money. Confirm payment arrangements directly with the other party.

Can I browse live listings on this website?+

This website introduces the app and uses fictional demo screens. Open the Android app to explore current published listings and their availability.

How do I get support or delete my account?+

Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} for support, or use Delete my account to prepare a deletion request. Never share a password or OTP.
