03 / BUY & SELL PROPERTY

Buying or selling?
Start with a conversation.

Houses, flats, shops, plots and land, from direct owners and agents. Every listing is checked before it goes live, and the seller decides when to share their number.

IF YOU’RE BUYING

  • Pick a city or All India, then filter by type, budget and direct owners
  • Sort by newest, price or lowest ₹ per sq ft
  • See photos, property details and the papers the seller has ready
  • Work out a home loan EMI right on the listing
  • Save up to 5 properties and see similar ones nearby
  • Chat, book a site visit, and call once the seller shares their number

IF YOU’RE SELLING

  • Post as an owner or as an agent or dealer, in 5 short steps
  • Add photos, price, area and the papers you have ready
  • Let AI draft the description, then review it
  • Keep your full address and phone number private
  • See every buyer inquiry and confirm visit times
  • Pause, renew or mark it sold. Listings stay live for 365 days

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before your
next move.

Talk to the team {{icon:arrow}}
Is SPoT only for rentals?+

No. SPoT started with rentals and now covers buying and selling too. Rent a home, room, PG or shop, buy or sell a house, flat, shop, plot or land, and manage tenants and rent records, all in the same Android app.

What can I find on SPoT?+

SPoT supports homes, rooms, PGs and shops for rent, plus houses, flats, shops, plots and land for sale. Listings and availability depend on what owners publish in your selected city. Explore current options in the Android app.

Can I manage my property as an owner?+

Yes. The Android app includes property and unit management, applications, tenant records, rent and electricity tracking, rent receipt sharing, rent agreement requests, rent due reminders for tenants, QR To-Let posters and a home-screen widget. See landlord tools.

Does reporting rent transfer money?+

Reporting or recording rent creates a payment record for review and tracking. It does not itself transfer money. Confirm payment arrangements directly with the other party.

Can I buy or sell a property on SPoT?+

Yes. Open the Buy tab in the Android app to browse houses, flats, shops, plots and land for sale in your city or across India. To sell, add a property and choose Sell a property. See Buy & Sell. Listings are checked before they go live and stay up for a year unless you mark them sold.

How do I contact a seller or visit the property?+

Tap Chat on a listing to message the seller in Inbox. The seller can choose to share their phone number with you. You can also request a site visit with up to 3 time slots, and the seller confirms one.

Does SPoT verify property papers or handle payment?+

No. SPoT helps buyers and sellers connect. Check ownership documents yourself, and never pay an advance before seeing the property and its papers. Report a listing in the app if something looks wrong.

Is the app available in Hindi?+

Yes. SPoT works in English and Hindi (हिंदी), including the Buy & Sell screens.

Can I browse live listings on this website?+

This website introduces the app and uses fictional demo screens. Open the Android app to explore current published listings and their availability.

Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} for support, or use Delete my account to prepare a deletion request. Never share a password or OTP.
