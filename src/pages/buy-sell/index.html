Compare better.

Open the Buy tab, pick your city or All India, and narrow it down.

{{icon:search}}

Search and filter

02 / CHAT FIRST, THEN VISIT

Ask questions.
Then go and see it.

Tap Chat on a listing to message the seller in Inbox. When you are ready, book a site visit from the same listing.

  • Propose up to 3 visit times and add a note for the owner.
  • The owner confirms one time. Both of you get a reminder 2 hours before.
  • Track every request in Visits: waiting, confirmed, declined or cancelled.
  • The seller can share their phone number with you in chat. Then you can call or copy it.

03 / SELL YOUR PROPERTY

Post it in five steps.
Stay in control.

Add a property and choose Sell a property. Owners, agents and dealers are all welcome.

  1. Type & basics

    Choose house, flat, shop, plot or land. Add a title, city, locality, asking price and area in sq ft, gaj, bigha, acre and more.

  2. Details & photos

    Add BHK, floors, road width, land use or frontage, tick the papers you have ready, and add photos. AI can draft the description for you to review.

  3. Review & go live

    Every listing and photo is checked before it goes live, usually within minutes. It then stays live for 365 days.

  4. Handle buyers

    See every buyer inquiry, share your number only with the buyers you choose, and confirm visit times.

  5. Keep it fresh

    Pause, resume, edit or renew any time. Every 90 days we ask if it is still for sale, and you can mark it sold.

Your full address and phone number are never shown on the listing. Share them in chat when you choose.

04 / STAY SAFE

A big decision
deserves care.

  1. See it in person. Visit the property and check the original papers before you pay anything.
  2. No advance to strangers. Never pay a token amount to someone you have not met. SPoT never asks buyers for advance payment.
  3. Keep bank details out of chat. Don’t share bank details, OTPs or passwords in messages.
  4. Report what looks wrong. Report fake details, an already sold property, a broker posing as owner, or anyone asking for money in advance.

BEFORE YOU BUY OR SELL

Buy & sell questions.
Clear answers.

Contact support {{icon:arrow}}
What kind of property can I buy or sell?+

Houses, flats, shops, plots and land. Listings come from owners and from agents or dealers, and agent listings carry a Broker label.

How do I contact a seller?+

Tap Chat on the listing to message the seller in Inbox. The seller can choose to share their phone number with you in that chat, and then you can call them.

How does booking a site visit work?+

Tap Book visit, propose up to 3 times and add an optional note. The owner confirms one time or declines. Once confirmed, both of you get a reminder 2 hours before the visit.

Is my phone number shown to everyone when I sell?+

No. Your number and full address are private. A buyer sees your number only after you tap Share my number in their chat. Sharing with a buyer can’t be undone for that buyer.

How long does my sale listing stay live?+

365 days after it goes live. Every 90 days the app asks if it is still for sale. You can pause, resume, renew for another year or mark it sold at any time.

Why was my listing not published?+

Listings and photos pass automatic checks first. Phone numbers, links or email addresses in the title or description, or a photo that breaks the photo policy, will hold a listing until you fix it and publish again.

Does SPoT verify papers or handle payment?+

No. Sellers tick the papers they say are ready, and SPoT does not check them. Check the originals yourself and arrange any payment directly. SPoT does not process payments.
