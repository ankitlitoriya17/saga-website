{{icon:building}}

Open your property

In your owner workspace, open the property actions for a published listing and choose Generate QR poster.

{{icon:key}}

Choose what to show

Pick Detailed or QR only, colour or black & white, and English, Hindi or both. The preview updates as you choose.

{{icon:chat}}

Print, save or share

Print on A4 at 100% / actual size, save the poster to your gallery, or share it on WhatsApp along with your listing link.

The poster is created on your phone. Only published listings can be turned into a poster, because tenants can only open published listings. QR posters are for rental listings; to sell a property, see Buy & Sell.

02 / TWO LAYOUTS

Shop window or
your own gate.

  • Detailed — property photo, type, locality, rent and the QR. Best for shops and notice boards.
  • QR only — TO-LET, a large QR, and the SPoT address. Rent and address stay off the poster, which suits your own gate.
  • Black & white — no colour fills, so cheap photocopies still scan.

QR only hides details from passers-by. Anyone who scans still sees your published listing; it does not make the listing private.

Poster par aapka phone number ya pura address kabhi nahi chhapta.

What does ₹999 include?+

One printed To-Let board of your chosen poster design, delivered to the address you enter. The price includes delivery. There are no advance payments; you pay ₹999 in cash on delivery.

Can I cancel my order?+

Yes, free of charge, from My board orders in the app while the order is placed or accepted — that is, until printing starts. Once printing has started the order can no longer be cancelled.

Why might an order be declined?+

We may decline an order we cannot deliver or confirm, for example an address outside our delivery area or a phone number we cannot reach. You see the reason in the app. Each property can have one open board order, and each account up to three.

Refunds and returns+

Because you pay only on delivery, nothing is charged for cancelled or declined orders. If there is a problem with your board, contact support with your order number and a photo.

What happens to my delivery details?+

Your name, phone and address are used only to confirm and deliver the order. They are visible to you and the SPoT team, and never appear on the board or in notifications. Read the privacy policy.

Kya poster banane ke paise lagte hain?+

Nahi. QR poster banana, print karna aur share karna free hai. Sirf printed board ke liye ₹999 lagte hain, jo delivery par cash mein dene hote hain.
